WHY ONLY THREE REWARD LAYOUTS

Ten bespoke reward screens is ten things to maintain. Every theme's collectible is one of three shapes: a collection you fill (Cats, Dogs, Horses, Music), a journey you advance along (Football, Space, Ocean), or an assembly that becomes one object (Dinosaurs, Racing, Robots). Art and nouns differ; layout and logic don't.

EARN RATE IS FIXED ACROSS THEMES

One reward per graded round that clears its predicted accuracy, plus one per level promotion. A journey stop, a fossil bone and a cat card cost exactly the same work — so switching worlds can never be a shortcut.
