Whizzo · mascot specification

Ten mascots, one set of rules

This is the commissioning document. A primitive-built v1 set already exists in Whizzo Mascots.dc.html — ten characters assembled from circles, rounded rectangles and triangles, good enough to ship ten themes now. Slots fall back to the striped placeholder only where v1 is not used, or in the reward-art sets that do not exist yet. Below: the geometry every version has to survive, the four states each mascot needs, and a per-theme brief specific enough to hand to an illustrator or an image model when you replace v1 with drawn work.

One drawing has to work at all four. 34px is the constraint that decides the design — if the silhouette is not recognisable as a filled shape at 34px, it is the wrong silhouette. Deliver SVG plus 2× PNG at each size.

Four states, no more

Idle Default. Awake, relaxed, looking at the learner.
Cheer Correct answer and reward screens. One pose only.
Thinking Question on screen. Attentive, never worried.
Resting Loading, empty states, nothing due today.

There is deliberately no sad or disappointed state. A wrong answer is expected at the edge of ability — a crestfallen mascot teaches a kid that working at their level is failing. Wrong answers get Thinking.

HOUSE STYLE — ALL TEN

Flat vector, no gradients, no outlines. Three to four flats per character plus one darker shade for form. Rounded terminals to match Outfit and the 20px radii. Chunky proportions: head roughly 45% of total height. Full body, feet visible, facing three-quarters toward the learner.

COLOR RULE

Each mascot's palette is built from its own theme accent plus paper and ink — never Spark orange, which belongs to the brand and to buttons. The mascot must read against its own tinted slot and against white, so no near-white body fills.

HARD NOS

No speech bubbles, no held signage, no school props (pencils, books, blackboards). No real team, brand or franchise resemblance. No spectacles or graduation caps. Nothing that has to be redrawn to localise.

Reward art is a separate commission

The mascot is one character per theme. The collectibles are a set: 24 cat cards, 18 tricks, 15 ribbons, 20 tracks, 8 fossil bones, 8 car parts, 8 bot parts, and 8 stops each for Football, Space and Ocean. That is roughly 145 small pieces — spec them at 108px in the same flat style, and ship them per theme so themes can launch one at a time.

Order of work

Ship the v1 primitive set across all ten themes now — it is consistent and it unblocks launch. Then commission drawn replacements in this order: Racing first, because a red triangular ear on a round red head fights being a fox and it is the weakest of the ten; then Cats, Space and Robots as the style test — youngest, middle and oldest of the range. If one illustrator holds across those three, they hold across all ten. Themes swap art independently, so drawn and primitive mascots can coexist in production, and any theme without art still falls back to the striped placeholder.
