HOW THE TEN WERE PICKED
Six interest families with no overlap — pets, sport, science, vehicles, arts, tech — so almost any kid finds one on the first screen and nobody has to scroll past nine near-misses.
AGE COVERAGE
Cats, Dogs, Dinosaurs read youngest. Football, Music, Robots and Racing hold up through high school. Space, Ocean and Horses span the middle. Every grade band has at least four credible choices.
WHAT A THEME MAY NEVER DO
Change the curriculum, the difficulty, or what earns a reward. A ribbon and a fossil are the same graded test underneath — otherwise switching themes becomes a way to farm easy wins.
